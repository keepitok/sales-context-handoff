# One evidence-rich deal walkthrough

Modified 2026-11-20 from Checkpoint GTM's deal-qualification-review; see [NOTICE.md](../../NOTICE.md).

Choose the sampled deal with the clearest existing evidence across origin, problem, exchanges, stakeholders and outcome. Do not automatically choose the largest or a win. Explain the selection in one sentence.

Write a documented-journey section in examples.md as a short narrative or dated bullets, alongside the selected outreach examples:
- How the opportunity began.
- The buyer's stated pain and expected outcome.
- Who participated and what decision roles are supported.
- Main objection, alternative and decision steps.
- Recorded result and any contradiction.
- Important missing evidence.

Cite each event. Preserve the distinction between an expected benefit and a realized outcome. If no deal has sufficient evidence, give the clearest partial walkthrough and state what is missing.

For noncommercial work, use one well-documented contact/account journey instead: why they were approached, exact exchanges, observed role or need, and separately verified conversion/status events. Include the observed profile/source-record link beside the name. Do not invent buyer pain, purchasing roles or a commercial decision for an event invitation. Explain which stages remain unknown.

Borrow the qualification review's "how do we know?" discipline, without its MEDDICC score, verdict, stage change or mandatory gap-closing actions. No new qualification framework is imposed on the user.
