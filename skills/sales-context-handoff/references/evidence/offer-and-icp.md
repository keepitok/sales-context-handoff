# Living offer and ICP drafts

At the start of collection, read the selected business website and relevant offer pages, then any supplied offer decks, pricing and existing ICP documents. Reuse material already captured during setup. Stay within the selected offers; this is business-context collection, not research into missing customer attributes. If a website is absent or inaccessible, use supplied documents or the user's explanation and record the limitation.

Save source extracts and evidence IDs before drafting these two files in `working/reports/`:

- `offer.md`: what is offered, the problem it addresses, promised benefit, delivery/scope, available pricing or commercial terms and constraints. Attribute promises to their source; they are not demonstrated customer outcomes.
- `icp.md`: the initially stated or implied audience, relevant company/contact characteristics, problems, situations and reasons the offer may fit. Label an audience inferred from website copy as a hypothesis. For noncommercial work, title its contents as an audience profile while keeping the filename.

Keep a short **Initial source-based draft** in each file with citations and source dates when known. This preserves the starting point. Maintain a separate **Current working definition**, initially marked preliminary. Do not invent missing details or ask the user to author an ICP. If an initial definition is not supportable, record what is known and the specific gap.

After each collection batch, update the current definitions only where new evidence supports a change. Add buyer language, demonstrated needs, observed attributes and outcomes; retain counterexamples and contradictions. Briefly explain material changes from the initial draft with evidence links. Do not overwrite a stated offer with one buyer's interpretation: distinguish what is promised, what buyers value and what outcomes are actually observed. For multiple offers, keep their definitions and associated evidence distinguishable within the same two files.

In `icp.md`, synthesize an explicit working profile rather than a loose list of observations: who appears to fit, which problems or circumstances matter and which observed conditions suggest poor fit. Separate company fit, contact role and timing. Use [ICP and signal guidance](hypotheses.md) for supporting examples, counterexamples and uncertainty. Every proposed criterion must be identified as a stated targeting choice, an evidence-supported observation or a hypothesis; unknowns remain visible. Cite named examples with observed profile/source links. Sparse evidence limits the definition; it does not justify invented thresholds or exclusions.

During Analyze, reconcile both drafts against the complete collected sample and user corrections. Keep supported criteria, qualify weak ones and explain unresolved contradictions. The result is an evidence-backed working definition, not a claim of a validated ICP or measured offer effectiveness. Retain these same files for review and handoff rather than creating competing final versions. Findings should link to them and summarize implications without repeating their full contents.
