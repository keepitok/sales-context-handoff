# Buyer roles from evidence

Modified 2026-11-20 from Anthropic's stakeholder-map; see [NOTICE.md](../../NOTICE.md).

Distinguish a title from a role in a specific decision. Capture known users, evaluators, champions, approvers, economic buyers and blockers using the user's own terminology when available.

A friendly contact is not necessarily a champion. Look for observed advocacy such as an internal introduction, sharing decision information or arranging access. A senior title alone does not establish budget authority. Procurement, contract signature and economic ownership can belong to different people.

Cite the message, call passage or recorded role behind each statement. Where only a seller's designation exists, retain that attribution. Leave role or stance blank when unsupported rather than guessing.

Write the buyer-roles section of icp.md with named, linked examples and summarize roles across the sampled deals without asserting a universal buying committee. Keep person-level roles distinct from company/customer fit criteria. For noncommercial outreach, describe observed participant or organizer roles without imposing a purchasing committee. Mention missing role evidence that matters to later targeting. Do not invent contacts, research new people, propose access paths or initiate relationship actions.
