# Lightweight ICP and signal hypotheses

Modified 2026-11-20 from zarif3624/define-icp; see [NOTICE.md](../../NOTICE.md).

Use only collected customer, deal, loss and conversation evidence. State sample size and limitations.

Separate company fit from contact role, intent and timing. For noncommercial outreach, assess audience fit against the agreed outcome rather than assuming commercial buyer criteria. Identify observed attributes or situations that plausibly affect the problem, ability to buy, participate or benefit. A won deal is not automatically a good-fit customer; retention and delivery quality may be unknown.

Keep three levels distinct: the user's documented ICP/intended audience, attributes actually observed in the sample, and hypotheses about why those attributes may matter. A seller's claim about a person's role or company remains attributed until supported by a record. Do not infer role or fit from a name, reply or profile URL alone.

For each useful hypothesis, write:
- What the evidence suggests and the named, linked supporting examples, including their observed outcomes.
- A named, linked counterexample when present, or a limitation when no comparison is available.
- Which missing attribute, evidence or enrichment would be needed to evaluate it.

Distinguish an externally observable signal from a private fact known only after a conversation. Do not imply that every observed buying trigger is available for prospecting.

Preserve existing qualification/scoring rules as current practice, with attribution. Do not invent numerical weights, thresholds, tiers, hard exclusions or a validated ICP. If the user has no recorded rule, leave the corresponding data empty and put the question in gaps.

No company enrichment, account scoring, market sizing or calibration. Record relevant missing inputs as gaps.

Always state what the evidence permits for ICP/audience-fit interpretation, even if the answer is that attributes or verified outcomes are insufficient. Do not invent a hypothesis to fill a section; identify the smallest useful next collection step instead.
