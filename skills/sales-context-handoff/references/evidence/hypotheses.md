# Lightweight ICP and signal hypotheses

Modified 2026-11-20 from zarif3624/define-icp; see [NOTICE.md](../../NOTICE.md).

Use only collected customer, deal, loss and conversation evidence. State sample size and limitations.

Separate company fit from buyer role, intent and timing. Identify observed attributes or situations that plausibly affect the problem, ability to buy or ability to benefit. A won deal is not automatically a good-fit customer; retention and delivery quality may be unknown.

For each useful hypothesis, write:
- What the evidence suggests and the supporting examples.
- A counterexample or limitation when present.
- Which missing attribute, evidence or enrichment would be needed to evaluate it.

Distinguish an externally observable signal from a private fact known only after a conversation. Do not imply that every observed buying trigger is available for prospecting.

Preserve existing qualification/scoring rules as current practice, with attribution. Do not invent numerical weights, thresholds, tiers, hard exclusions or a validated ICP. If the user has no recorded rule, leave the corresponding data empty and put the question in gaps.

No company enrichment, account scoring, market sizing or calibration. Record relevant missing inputs as gaps.
